# Motivation
With more students suffering from shorter attention spans with the rise of short-form content in the digital sphere, it has spilled over its repercussions into our study habits. Students are now more easily distracted due to its addictive nature, which makes it harder for them to complete assignments on time and fully understand the content of their syllabus without their full attention. If left unaddressed, these challenges can result in long-term academic decline, increased stress, and hindered personal growth

# Aim
Our goal is to empower students to regain control over their focus when they study and cultivate healthier study habits by providing an app which is simple yet effective. We aim to develop an app that helps students build an awareness and discipline in managing their phone usage during study periods. By proactively addressing the issue of digital distractions, we seek to prevent its long-term negative effects on students’ academic performance and mental well-being

# User Stories
1. As a student, who wants to cultivate better studying habits, I want my phone to warn me when I am about to pick up my phone to distract myself away from my studies. 
2. As a student, who struggles with procrastination, I want an app that helps me schedule focused study periods with reminders to take breaks, so I can manage my time effectively.
3. As a student, who is motivated by progress, I want to earn points and level up by completing study sessions, so that I can feel rewarded for staying focused and building consistent habits.

```bash

```
### 2. Install dependencies

```bash
npm install
```
### 3. Start the development server

```bash
npx expo start
```
